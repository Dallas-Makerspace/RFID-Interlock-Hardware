The Interlock senses valve operation instead of current.  Two OPTO22 G4IAC5 opto interfaces sense the 110V coil drive and provide the Pi controller with 3.3V logic levels.  
The 5V IAC5 output is converted to 3.3V out with a 6.8K resistor fom each output to ground. The two outputs are diode-or'd to drive GPIO27 (same as standard current sense.) No software modificaton is required.
